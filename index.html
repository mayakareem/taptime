The Opportunity

The Middle East banking sector is ready for a new training partner. We're it.

Banks across the region spend heavily on training each year, and they want real operators in the room - not academics, not consultants. TapTime brings the GCC's top banking builders to teach the next generation of leaders, and turns every cohort into a self-compounding expert network.

Why now

Three structural shifts open this window. The first to move owns the segment.

1

Banks are spending. Incumbents are stretched.

UAE Tier 1 banks invest meaningfully in L&D every year. EIBFS handles scale-banker training well, but it's not built for C-suite peer learning. The gap between what banks pay for and what they actually want is wide and growing.

2

The operator network is reachable.

Ex-CEOs of Wio, Liv., Mashreq Neo. Founders of Tabby, Tamara, Lean, Tarabut. Ex-CDOs and CROs from FAB, ENBD, Mashreq. The exact people banks want to learn from are reachable through founder-led warm intros, not ad budgets.

3

The flywheel compounds.

Every cohort onboards a handful of marquee experts to TapTime as paid marketplace listings. Each new cohort adds banking experts who compound network effects. No pure training company can replicate this. We are the only operator-network plus cohort hybrid.

The product

Three cohort tiers. One reusable spine. Faculty rotates on every run.

Same curriculum runs four to six times a year with different operator lineups. Buyers see this as a feature - they get multi-bank pattern recognition, not one professor's POV. From CEO to RM, we have a cohort for every layer of the bank.

The first move

One bank. One cohort. One country. The wedge that compounds.

We start with a single, focused win. The same playbook then repeats across banks and across tiers, with every new cohort adding faculty depth and reference customers.

A focused Tier 2 cohort priced for L&D approval without board sign-off. CDO transformation is the hottest topic in ME banking right now. EIBFS doesn't run a comparable program. Faculty for it is the easiest tier to recruit. Mashreq's innovation lineage with Liv. and Mashreq Neo makes them the natural first buyer.

Two paths to revenue
